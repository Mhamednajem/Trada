Welcome to PeetX Ecosystem

Follow this detailed guide to set up your non-custodial decentralized Web3 wallet, connect via the official smart contract endpoint, and start accumulating daily returns with absolute security.

Step-by-Step Onboarding Architecture

1
Install Web3 Wallet

Download a secure Web3 non-custodial wallet such as Trust Wallet, MetaMask, or TokenPocket on your mobile device or browser.

2
Access DApp Browser

Open your wallet application and navigate to the internal decentralized Web3 Browser (DApp browser) section.

3
Enter Official Gateway

Paste the verified ecosystem URL (peetx.app) into the top address bar to load the decentralized interface directly.

4
Authorize Contract & Yield

Click 'Connect Wallet' to execute the secure smart contract interaction and begin generating daily automated yields.
