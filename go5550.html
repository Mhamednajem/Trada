How does financial sharing through referrals work?
Profits depend on mutual financial sharing via smart contract (USDT min $10 per referral). Upon a successful transaction, profits are distributed automatically: 30 generations above and 30 generations below you, providing permanent referral links secured by the smart contract.
What happens when the contract reaches 25,000 users?
Once the contract reaches 25,000 users, standard registration closes. However, this unlocks a special feature allowing you to purchase referrals where $1 gives you 10 referrals ($1 for every 10 referrals). Every new user joining through your link grants you generation profits (from 1st to 5th generation) which are automatically credited to your team section with identical profit rates and wallet-supported mechanisms.
What is the 30 Generations Above & Below System?
It features 30 generations above and 30 generations below your node, ensuring fully interconnected and mutually distributed profits among active participants.
Do profits depend on investment volume size?
No! Sharing profits do not depend on the investment volume size, guaranteeing absolute equity and fairness for everyone in the network.
Is the system sustainable for the long term?
Yes! It features a sustainable, infinite, and permanent system designed to operate forever through the smart contract.
